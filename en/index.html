Pixline privacy policy

Version of 10 November 2026

Pixline is a puzzle game for Android. This policy describes what data the game processes. In short: the game does not collect, send or share any data, and I have no access to it.

Controller

Maciej Libucha, an individual, not acting as a business.
Contact: maciejlibucha01@gmail.com

What data is stored in the game

The game stores only the data it needs to play, on your device:

  • campaign progress (completed levels, current level, whether the tutorial is done, number of hints),
  • settings (vibration, animations).

This data stays in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to me or to any other company. You can delete it by resetting progress in the game settings or by uninstalling the game.

Google backup

The game lets Android include its saved data (progress and settings) in your Google account backup and in transfers to a new phone. Android does this according to your backup settings, and the data is stored by Google. You can turn this off in system settings (Google, Backup). I have no access to these backups.

What the game does not do

  • It does not show ads.
  • It has no internet access and sends no data off your device.
  • It needs no account or registration.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting or third-party trackers.
  • It does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It only uses vibration, unless you turn it off.

Children

The game is not directed at children. It does not collect any data, including from children.

Your rights

Because I do not process your personal data, I have no data to give you access to, correct or delete. If you have questions, write to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is processed, for example by adding ads, I will update this policy before releasing that version. The current version date is at the top of the page.
